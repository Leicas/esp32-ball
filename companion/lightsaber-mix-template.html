Audio routing and protection

The 24-frame look-ahead limiter adds 0.5 ms and caps output at 30,100 / 32,767 (91.9% digital full scale), with a roughly 100 ms full-scale gain recovery. Requested gains may be reduced during loud combinations. No hard-clipped boost is applied.

What was measured, and what is estimated

At 500 Hz, MF + HF at half scale each measured 1.99 g fundamental with matching polarity and 3.99 g with HF reversed. This supports the opposite-polarity motion layer. It does not establish the best phase at other frequencies.

Acoustic measurements use the Mad Catz microphone at 10 cm and are uncalibrated dBFS. Severe upper-band attenuation may include microphone processing. The combined estimate is a root-sum-square magnitude model, not a phase-aware prediction or guaranteed SPL. It excludes limiter action. IMU results describe motion at the sensor, not the whole enclosure. LFi pulse harmonics are intentional.

Negative LFi test: at 65 Hz, total acceleration was 127 mg RMS at quarter drive, 252 mg at half drive, and 1,013 mg at full drive. The nonlinear jump rules out full negative drive for the clean preset. Installed negative rumble is 35–50% peak, with positive clashes overriding it, followed by recovery and a fade back.
